# Bamboo Tip-Grab pump is Lean + Anti-damping Boost, not Tarzan equilibrium-switch

The original tip-grab tilt model was Tarzan-style: holding A/D applied a constant lean torque whose magnitude was tuned so the steady-state `BendAngle` settled at `grabHoldBendDeg`. Amplitude growth required the player to flip A↔D **at the swing extremes** so the new equilibrium target sat across rest from the current position; well-timed flips amplified amplitude per cycle, mistimed flips killed it. We replaced this with a two-channel model: the lean torque stays (now called **Lean**) and a new **Pump Boost** channel injects energy proportional to `|angularVel|` whenever the held input direction matches the tip's motion direction. The soft cap at `grabMaxDeflectionDeg` is now a quartic stiffening torque inside the ODE instead of a hard `ω=0` clamp.

## Why

- **Tarzan timing is unforgiving.** With `grabDampingRatio = 0.05` and `chargingTiltScale = 1`, the pump only grew amplitude when the player flipped A↔D within ~50 ms of a swing extreme. In playtest the feel was "doesn't grow, just damps down according to damping" — exactly the symptom the player reported. The new model rewards a much more intuitive pattern: hold the direction the tip is already moving in, and the swing grows.
- **The "pole feel" gap the player named was acceleration, not gravity.** Initial diagnosis suggested adding slime-weight gravity torque (pendulum mode). The player rejected that — they want the bamboo to spring back to upright when no input is held, not droop under load. So the missing ingredient is **acceleration during input**, which `boost = |input|·ω·gain·I` provides directly (gain in 1/s units, scales with current speed so the kick gets stronger as the swing builds, matching the "playground swing pump" mental model).
- **Lean + Boost are orthogonal, both useful.** Lean alone gives a static bend when the player just rests on the input — visually communicating "the bamboo bends under my input" even before any swinging. Boost alone has no effect at `ω = 0`. Keeping both means the bamboo responds both to a light touch (lean) and to swing-momentum-aware input (boost), without one collapsing into the other.
- **Hard `ω=0` clamp felt like hitting a wall.** With pump amplification routinely driving the bamboo to `grabMaxDeflectionDeg`, the velocity wipe at the cap was visible as a jerk and broke the "elastic stalk" illusion. The quartic penalty inside the ODE keeps the cap inviolate (penalty → ∞ as `|θ| → cap`) while letting the existing spring + damping smoothly decelerate the swing across the last ~30 % of the range.

## Consequences

- **Charging tilt scales default to 0.** The launch sequence (charge button + A/D for aim direction in some configs) shares the A/D axis with tilt; allowing tilt during Charging means the player accidentally pumps the bamboo while aiming. Both `chargingTiltScale` and `grabPumpChargingScale` default to 0; the designer can enable either if a specific stalk wants tilt-during-charge.
- **`grabPumpBoostGain > 2ζω` produces exponential growth until the soft cap.** This is the intended behavior — sustained matched input always saturates at the cap. Designers who want a stable upper-bound amplitude can lower the boost gain below the damping coefficient (`2 · grabDampingRatio · 2π · grabSpringFreqHz`) so net damping stays positive and amplitude tops out at a `pumpGain / dampingCoef` ratio instead.
- **Side-attach behavior is unchanged.** The soft cap, the boost channel, and the lean default change all gate on `IsGrabbedByCachedSlime()`. A slime hopping off a bent stalk still gets the predictable `ω=0` clamp at `maxDeflectionDeg` it always had.
- **Numerical-noise threshold on boost.** Boost is suppressed when `|ω| < 0.05 rad/s` so sign-of-ω flicker near zero doesn't kick the bamboo into a spurious direction. Lean still fires below that threshold so the player can always start the swing from rest.
- **Reaction Boost (launch carrier inheritance) is unchanged in formula but stronger in practice.** Because saturated swings now reach `grabMaxDeflectionDeg` reliably, the peak `AngularVelocity` at the `BendAngle ≈ 0` crossing is larger than under the Tarzan model. Designers may want to lower `grabModeCarrierScale` if launches feel too powerful.
